Features

Everything a club match needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Built for real matches on real courts: big targets you can hit between points, the proper rules, and a summary that's worth reading afterwards.

Score from your wrist

Tap YOU or OPP to give the point. Undo is one tap away, and Double Tap on newer watches awards you the point without touching the screen.

The proper rules

Best of 1, 3 or 5. Advantage or no-ad, tie-breaks to 7 or 10, match tie-breaks, serve rotation and change of ends, singles or doubles.

A real Tennis workout

Every match runs as a Tennis workout in Apple Health, with heart rate, active calories, steps and distance. Indoor courts are saved as indoor tennis.

Haptics you can feel

A different tap for a point, game, set, match and change of ends, and a double tap when it's your serve. Keep your eyes on the ball.

Rivals & partners

Name your opponents and doubles partners. Each rival gets a full head-to-head: record, form, tie-breaks, biggest win and their favourite excuse.

Stats that tell the story

Record, form, streaks, serve and return, break points, tie-breaks, deciding sets and comebacks, filtered by 30 days, 90 days, a year or all time.

Weather & venue

The conditions for the hours you played and the nearest club are filled in for you. Find out whether you really do play better when it's mild and calm.

Padel too

Switch sport and Love-40 goes doubles with golden point on astro, adds "The glass" to the excuses, and talks padel in the commentary.

Share, export, back up

Share a result card, export your matches to CSV, and keep an automatic backup in the Files app that you can restore at any time.

FAQ

Good questions.

Which devices do I need?

An Apple Watch on watchOS 10 or later, paired with an iPhone on iOS 17 or later. Scoring happens on the watch; the iPhone app is where you see your history, stats and live scoreboard. Double Tap scoring needs Apple Watch Series 9 or Ultra 2 with watchOS 11.

Does it work without my iPhone nearby?

Yes. The watch scores and records the workout on its own. Finished matches sync to your iPhone the next time they're in range.

Does it work for doubles?

Yes. At the start of each set the watch asks each team who serves first, then tracks who's serving every game and tie-break point. Stats show service games held per player and per partner.

Do I have to give it access to Health?

No. Without Health access the scoring works exactly the same; you just won't get heart rate, calories, steps or a workout saved to Health.

What happens if I score a point by mistake?

Tap the undo arrow at the top of the score screen. You can undo as far back as you like, and the serve, ends and stats all follow.

Can I back up my matches?

Yes. The iPhone app keeps an automatic backup in the Files app (On My iPhone › Love-40), and you can export a backup to iCloud Drive or anywhere else from Defaults › Backup. Restoring only adds what's missing, so it never overwrites your history.
